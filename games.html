A selection of games exploring story, systems and the ways players can shape their experience.

Reel Feelings!

A six-day visual novel about getting to know three very unusual people - and deciding how much you're willing to believe them. Build relationships through conversation, explore each character's story and take to the water with an original fishing minigame.

I designed and developed the project across narrative, branching choices, relationship systems and gameplay, using Ren'Py alongside Python and Pygame.

Role:
Game Designer, Narrative Designer, Programmer

Koa: The Lost Realms

A collaborative puzzle-platformer created by an 80-student development team, Koa: The Lost Realms follows one tiny explorer through a forgotten kingdom of snowy peaks, shifting sands, and mossy ruins.

I contributed to the early concept of Koa and worked as an Art Producer, helping coordinate the art team and keep creative work moving through production. The project taught me how design decisions, creative dependencies and production constraints interact within a large collaborative team.

Role: Art Producer, Producer Liason

Shrimpyscroller

What if a pistol shrimp was the hero of a shoot-'em-up? Shrimpyscroller is a short, comedic shmup that swaps spaceships and guns for one very angry little crustacean and its ridiculously powerful sonic boom.

My first game made in Unity and my Foundation Year final project, developed entirely as a solo project. I designed the gameplay loop and levels, scripted the game, and handled the integration of art and music assets.

Role: Game Designer, Programmer, Level Designer

Spare Parts

A text-based exploration game and my first project using C#. Players control a damaged robot searching a mysterious facility for missing parts while solving riddles to earn their escape.

Some Postman

What happens when a postman starts stealing the love letters he's supposed to deliver? Some Postman is a short, silly Twine game where keeping yourself happy means risking getting caught.

Made for the "My First Game Jam" before I started university, the game uses simple resource and consequence systems to create a balancing act between happiness and suspicion. Push either too far and your career takes an unexpectedly disastrous turn.

Role: Game Designer, Narrative Designer, Programmer
