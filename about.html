About Me

I am a game designer and developer interested in creating meaningful interactive experiences through strong design, storytelling, and player engagement.

Background

My background includes narrative design, gameplay development, programming, and project management across both solo and collaborative projects. I enjoy exploring how mechanics, systems, and narrative can work together to create memorable player experiences.

Through my work, I have developed experience in designing branching narratives, implementing gameplay features, building user interfaces, and taking projects from initial concept through to playable prototypes and completed releases.
