About Me

I'm a game designer and developer interested in the space between story and play. I enjoy creating games where characters, choices and gameplay systems work together to make the player feel involved in the story - not just watch it unfold.

My Background

My experience spans narrative design, gameplay development, programming and project management across both solo and collaborative projects. I enjoy taking an idea from an early concept and figuring out how it can become something players can actually interact with.

I've worked on branching narratives, character-driven experiences, gameplay systems and user interfaces, as well as the practical implementation needed to turn those ideas into playable projects. I'm particularly interested in understanding how each part of a game can support the experience as a whole.

What I'm interested in

I'm particularly interested in:

  • Narrative and game design
  • Interactive storytelling
  • Player choice and branching narratives
  • Gameplay systems that support narrative
  • Character-driven experiences
  • User experience and interface design
  • Independent game development
