Shrimpyscroller

X Statement

Razor Statement

Shrimpyscroller is a 2D side-scrolling shoot 'em up in which players take control of a rebellious prawn taking on an oppressive government. Developed as my first Unity project, the game provided an introduction to the full development process, from initial concept and design through to programming, testing, and release.
As a solo developer, I was responsible for all aspects of production, including game design, programming, art integration, balancing, and project management. The project gave me practical experience working within technical limitations, prioritising achievable scope, and completing a game from start to finish.
The experience reinforced the importance of designing projects that match available resources and development experience, while still delivering a complete and enjoyable player experience.
