Findex

Marine Species & Environmental Observation Database

A catalogue of marine species, fishing observations, and unusual aquatic life recorded around Beach Town.

Findex is a marine database documenting species observed in and around the waters of Beach Town. Records include common local species as well as less frequently encountered specimens.

The database records observations made by local fishers and other members of the community. Entries may include information about rarity, behaviour, and notable physical characteristics.

Some species recorded in the area display unusual traits or adaptations. The reasons for these variations are not always understood, and observations are subject to ongoing research.

Notable Observations

Three-Eyed Perch

A rare perch displaying an additional eye. Reports suggest unusual responsiveness to movement and changes in its surroundings.

Transparent Carp

A rare carp with an unusually translucent body. Specimens are difficult to observe and appear particularly sensitive to disturbances in the water.

Backwards Pike

A rare pike exhibiting unusual movement behaviour. Attempts to reel in the species may produce responses opposite to those normally expected.

Rarity

Findex currently uses five rarity classifications:

  • Common — Frequently encountered in local waters.
  • Uncommon — Present in the area but encountered less frequently.
  • Rare — Infrequently observed and more difficult to encounter.
  • Very Rare — Exceptional sightings with a very low encounter rate.
  • Mythical — Extremely unusual records with very limited observations.

Behaviour

Species recorded by Findex may display different behaviours when caught. These behaviours affect how they respond during retrieval and may require different techniques from the fisher.

  • Steady — Relatively predictable movement.
  • Aggressive — Stronger and more forceful movement.
  • Erratic — Unpredictable changes in movement.
  • Predictive — Movement appears to follow a recognisable pattern.
  • Nervous — Particularly sensitive and prone to sudden movement.
  • Inverted — Responds contrary to conventional retrieval expectations.

Environmental Notes

Local observations have recorded changes in the marine environment around Beach Town, including altered habitats, visible pollution, and unusual biological characteristics.

The relationship between these observations is currently under investigation. Existing records suggest that the changes have developed over an extended period rather than resulting from a single recent event.

Further observations are required before the full extent or cause of these changes can be established.

Database Notes

Findex records are based on observations and should not be considered a complete scientific survey of the local marine ecosystem.

Species classifications and behavioural descriptions may be updated as additional observations become available.
