info

A popular finfluencer known for her polished image, upbeat personality, and carefully curated view of life in Beach Town.

Roselyn Suwan is a betta fish reverse mermaid and social media personality based in Beach Town. She creates lifestyle and local-interest content for an online audience, with a particular focus on the town's cafés, attractions, seaside views, and everyday life.

Roselyn is well known around Beach Town and is accustomed to being recognised by her followers. Her public persona is confident, cheerful, and highly polished, making her a familiar presence at many of the town's more social locations.

Although much of her content focuses on the positive side of Beach Town, Roselyn is also aware of the environmental changes affecting the area. Her approach to discussing these issues has become a subject of interest as the condition of the local environment becomes increasingly difficult to ignore.

personality

Roselyn is outgoing, charismatic, and socially confident. She is comfortable talking to strangers, maintaining conversations, and adapting her behaviour to different social situations. She has a strong understanding of what people respond to and is skilled at presenting herself in an appealing way.

She is creative and observant, with a talent for finding interesting details in otherwise ordinary places. Roselyn also tends to be empathetic and attentive to the reactions of people around her, although this can sometimes lead her to prioritise keeping others happy over expressing her own opinions.

Roselyn dislikes unnecessary confrontation and generally prefers to smooth over disagreements rather than escalate them. She is particularly careful about how she presents herself, both online and in person, and is known for maintaining a positive attitude even when circumstances are less than ideal.

relationships

FRIENDS

Molly May
Roselyn and Molly have very different approaches to life in Beach Town. Molly is known for speaking openly about environmental problems, while Roselyn is more cautious about how such subjects are presented to the public. Despite their differences, the two share a genuine concern for the town.
Mitchell Cawley
Roselyn and Mitchell are both known for their upbeat public personalities. Their friendship is generally relaxed and friendly, although their reasons for maintaining such positive attitudes are not always the same.

ROMANTIC INTEREST

The Player
Roselyn meets the Player during their time in Beach Town. What begins as casual time spent together gradually develops into a closer relationship as the Player gets to know her away from her public persona.

ASSOCIATES

Beach Town community
As a local finfluencer, Roselyn has connections throughout Beach Town and is familiar with many of its businesses, attractions, and residents.

story

Roselyn was originally a betta fish before becoming a reverse mermaid. Like other reverse mermaids, the exact circumstances and mechanism behind her transformation are not fully understood.

Her memories of her life as a betta are fragmented and sometimes difficult to distinguish from later recollections. As a result, Roselyn does not always have a clear picture of her earliest experiences.

In modern-day Beach Town, Roselyn has built a successful online presence around her personality and her life in the town. Her content focuses heavily on attractive locations, local experiences, and the positive aspects of seaside life.

Her growing audience has made her one of the more recognisable young residents of Beach Town. However, maintaining a public image requires considerable attention to how she speaks, what she shares, and how other people perceive her.

SPOILER WARNING

Over the course of the story, Roselyn's carefully maintained public image becomes increasingly difficult to separate from her private feelings. Her relationship with the Player gives her an opportunity to be seen without relying entirely on the persona she presents to her audience.

Roselyn's story ultimately concerns the difference between being liked by an audience and being genuinely known by another person. As the environmental situation in Beach Town becomes harder to ignore, she must decide how much of herself she is willing to show when presenting an honest version of events may come at a personal cost.
