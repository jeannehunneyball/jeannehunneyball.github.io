info

A friendly and energetic local fitness enthusiast known for encouraging everyone around him to keep moving, keep smiling, and keep going.

Mitchell Cawley, usually known as Mitch, is a blobfish reverse mermaid living in Beach Town. He is well known locally for his enthusiasm for fitness, his friendly personality, and his tendency to encourage the people around him.

Mitch is particularly active around the town's recreational spaces and has developed a reputation for being approachable and supportive. He often takes on a helping role in social situations and is quick to offer encouragement when someone is struggling.

As a blobfish reverse mermaid, Mitch has a distinctive physical adaptation to changes in pressure. His body naturally changes depending on his environment, allowing him to exist at different depths despite the considerable differences between deep water and the surface.

personality

Mitch is energetic, friendly, and naturally encouraging. He enjoys helping other people and tends to approach problems with an optimistic attitude. He is comfortable making jokes, starting conversations, and keeping the mood light when situations become uncomfortable.

He is disciplined and enjoys maintaining routines, particularly when it comes to exercise and fitness. Mitch takes pride in being dependable and is often more concerned with whether the people around him are doing well than with his own needs.

Mitch generally prefers practical solutions to emotional conversations. He has a tendency to treat difficult situations as problems that can be overcome through persistence, positivity, or simply keeping busy. While this makes him resilient in many circumstances, it can also make it difficult for him to acknowledge when something cannot simply be pushed through.

relationships

FRIENDS

Molly May
Mitch and Molly have noticeably different approaches to difficult situations. Molly is more inclined to confront problems directly, while Mitch tends to adapt and keep moving forward. Despite this difference, they have a mutual respect for one another.
Roselyn Suwan
Mitch and Roselyn are both familiar with maintaining upbeat public personalities. Their approaches are different, however, with Roselyn carefully managing her public image while Mitch tends to rely on constant positivity and activity.

ROMANTIC INTEREST

The Player
Mitch meets the Player during their time in Beach Town. Their relationship gradually becomes more personal as the Player gets to know Mitch beyond his usual cheerful and encouraging personality.

ASSOCIATES

Beach Town community
Mitch is a familiar and well-liked member of the local community. His willingness to help others and his involvement in fitness and recreational activities have given him connections throughout town.

story

Mitch was originally a blobfish before becoming a reverse mermaid. His memories of his life as a marine animal are incomplete, although he retains impressions and feelings associated with parts of his past.

His unusual appearance is partly the result of the pressure differences between his natural deep-water environment and the surface. At greater pressure, Mitch's body becomes compressed, producing his more streamlined, blue appearance. At the surface, where the pressure is considerably lower, his body expands into a softer, pinker form.

These are natural physical states rather than separate identities, and Mitch remains the same person in either form.

In Beach Town, Mitch has built a life around fitness, routine, and helping other people. He is generally regarded as an optimistic and dependable presence in the community.

SPOILER WARNING

As the story progresses, Mitch's usual approach to difficult situations becomes harder to maintain. His instinct is to keep busy, stay positive, and adapt to whatever happens, but some problems cannot be solved simply by trying harder.

His relationship with the Player gradually gives him space to acknowledge feelings that he normally avoids. Rather than learning to become stronger or more resilient, Mitch's story is about recognising that he does not always have to be those things for everyone else.
