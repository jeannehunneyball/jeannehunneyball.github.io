info

A retired environmental activist with a long memory, a strong opinion, and considerably less patience than she used to have.

Molly May is a goldfish reverse mermaid and former environmental activist associated with Beach Town. She is known for her extensive knowledge of the town's history and surrounding marine environment.

Molly was previously involved in environmental campaigns concerning the condition of Beach Town's coastline and surrounding waters. Although she is no longer as publicly active as she once was, she continues to take a keen interest in changes to the local environment.

She is particularly knowledgeable about the history of the town and its waters, and is often among the first to recognise changes that others have overlooked.

story

Molly lived as an ordinary goldfish before becoming a reverse mermaid. She retains relatively strong memories of her earlier life, including memories of Beach Town before many of the environmental changes now visible along its coastline.

Over the course of her life, Molly became increasingly involved in environmental activism. She campaigned against damage to the local marine environment and became a familiar figure in discussions surrounding pollution, habitat degradation, and changes to the waters around Beach Town.

Her activism eventually became a significant part of her identity. Molly collected newspaper clippings, campaign materials, and other records relating to environmental causes, and developed an extensive knowledge of the town's environmental history.

RETIREMENT FROM ACTIVISM

Molly is no longer as publicly involved in environmental campaigning as she once was. Although the reasons for her withdrawal are not widely discussed, she remains highly attentive to changes in the surrounding waters.

Recent reports of unusual marine behaviour and changing fishing grounds have renewed Molly's interest in the condition of Beach Town's environment.

SPOILER WARNING

Molly's history with Beach Town's environmental decline is more personal than she initially lets on. Her memories of the town before its decline have shaped much of her understanding of what is happening beneath its waters.

Although she once believed strongly in the possibility of meaningful change, years of activism have left Molly questioning whether her efforts made any difference.

Her story in Reel Feelings! concerns not simply whether Beach Town can be changed, but whether Molly can allow herself to hope that change is still possible.
