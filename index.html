Designing games where story and play work together.

I'm a game designer and developer interested in the space between narrative and gameplay - creating characters, choices and systems that give players a reason to care about what happens next.

My work combines narrative design, gameplay systems and hands-on implementation, with a particular focus on player choice, character-driven experiences and mechanics that support the story rather than simply sit alongside it.

Featured Project

Reel Feelings!

A six-day visual novel about relationships, secrets and the things we choose to believe. Players get to know three unusual characters through branching conversations, while an original fishing minigame adds another layer to exploration, pacing and worldbuilding.

Developed as a solo project, Reel Feelings! combines narrative design, gameplay systems and technical implementation in Ren'Py, with the fishing minigame developed in Python and Pygame.

Role:
Game Designer, Narrative Designer, Programmer

Highlights:

  • Branching character routes and multiple endings
  • Relationship and player-choice systems
  • Original fishing minigame built with Python/Pygame
  • Integrated gameplay systems into Ren'Py
