Reel Feelings!

A visual novel about relationships, secrets and the things we choose to believe.

Reel Feelings! is a six-day visual novel where players build relationships with three unusual characters through branching conversations, exploration and an original fishing minigame. Developed as a solo project, the game combines narrative design, gameplay systems and technical implementation to make player choices matter both mechanically and emotionally.

Project Overview

Reel Feelings! was my final major project, developed as a solo game from concept through to a playable release.

Role:
Game Designer, Narrative Designer, Programmer

Tools:
Ren'Py, Python, Pygame, Twine, Git

Format:
Six-day visual novel with branching routes and an optional fishing minigame

Design Approach

Reel Feelings! was designed around the idea that gameplay should contribute to the emotional experience rather than simply sit alongside the narrative.

Key Systems

The game combines branching dialogue, relationship values, shared awareness and character-specific traits to shape the player's relationships and determine the outcome of each route.

Development

I developed the project from concept through implementation, working across narrative structure, gameplay systems, UI, programming and integration.

Reflection

Developing Reel Feelings! as a solo project gave me the opportunity to explore how narrative, systems and implementation can influence one another throughout development.
